# Gun Viewmodel — First-Person Back View

## Goal

The current pistol is drawn in *side profile* — slide horizontal across the
top, grip slanting down-left, barrel exiting the right edge. That was the
original `gun-viewmodel` spec's contract (`AC #2`: "stylized pistol silhouette
pointing slightly up-right with the grip at the bottom-left of the sprite, the
slide horizontal across the upper half, the barrel exit at roughly
`(GUN_W - 8, 16)`"). It reads as a 2D arcade gun, not a gun the player is
holding.

Re-author the sprite in **first-person back view**: the player is looking
*along* the gun toward the target, so the slide is foreshortened *into* the
screen, the grip is at the bottom of the sprite (closest to the camera), and
the muzzle exits at the *far end* of the slide near the top of the sprite.
Pose: canted slightly toward screen-right (Doom-style — held in the
right hand, gun shifted to the lower-right quadrant of the screen, slide
axis tipped slightly so the muzzle points toward upper-left of the gun's own
sprite-local frame). Still **no hands** — the gun appears to float, exactly
as in the current implementation.

The bob, fire-kick, dry-fire, muzzle-flash, death-freeze-hides-the-gun, and
hitscan integration paths (`onPlayerFire('shot' | 'dry')`,
`tickKick`, `getKickOffsets`, `bobPhase` / `bobIntensity` math) are all
unchanged in behavior — only the sprite, the screen anchor, and the
barrel-exit point shift.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Single-file constraint preserved.** Still one `index.html` at the repo
   root, no build step, no external assets, no network requests, no
   `localStorage`. All sprite pixels are generated by JS at startup.

2. **Sprite is procedurally generated once at startup.** `GUN_SPRITE` stays
   a `Uint32Array(GUN_W * GUN_H)` of packed RGBA where alpha is `0`
   (transparent — skip in blit) or `255` (opaque). **`Math.random()` is
   forbidden** in `buildGun()` — the byte-identical-first-frame contract
   from the original spec (`gun-viewmodel` AC #3) carries over verbatim.
   `FLASH_FRAME_1` and `FLASH_FRAME_2` are unchanged (they're radial /
   ray bursts and read correctly from any angle).

3. **`GUN_W` and `GUN_H` may change.** The current `GUN_W = 96`,
   `GUN_H = 80` were sized for a horizontal side profile. A back view is
   naturally taller than wide. Pick proportions appropriate for the new
   silhouette — recommended `GUN_W = 72`, `GUN_H = 96` (a 4:3 portrait
   footprint) but the implementer is free to tune within
   `[64..96] × [80..104]`. Update the constants at `index.html:72-73`
   and verify `gunFillRect` / `gunOutlineRect` still bounds-check correctly
   (they read `GUN_W` / `GUN_H`, so changing the constants is enough).

4. **Sprite layout — back view.** The visible silhouette reads top-to-bottom
   (far-end → near-end of the gun), not left-to-right:

   ```
                         [GUN_W]
       0 ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
         │                                   │
         │          ╔═══╗  ← rear sight      │
         │          ║ ▪ ║                    │  far end
         │     ╔════╩═══╩════╗               │  (muzzle, into screen)
         │     ║   slide    ║                │
         │     ║   (top of  ║                │
         │     ║   gun, fore-                │
         │     ║   shortened)║                │
         │     ╚════════════╝                │
         │   ╔════════════════╗              │
         │   ║   frame /       ║             │
         │   ║   trigger guard ║             │
         │   ║   (visible from  ║            │
         │   ║   above-rear)    ║            │
         │   ╚════════════════╝              │
         │     ╔════════════╗                │
         │     ║   grip     ║                │
         │     ║  (held by  ║                │  near end
         │     ║   invisible ║               │  (player's hand,
         │     ║   right hand)║               │   off-screen below)
       H └─────╚════════════╝────────────────┘
   ```

   Specifically:
   - **Grip** sits at the bottom of the sprite (the part closest to the
     camera). Vertical or near-vertical, brown checkered. Bottom edge can
     extend slightly past `GUN_H` (clipped by the existing `py >= H` bounds
     check) so the player's hand reads as off-screen — same trick the
     current sprite uses with `gunY = H - GUN_H + 4`.
   - **Frame / trigger guard** above the grip. Visible because the camera
     looks slightly down at the gun (rear-three-quarters view, not pure
     top-down). Trigger guard reads as an outlined hollow shape; the
     trigger itself is an internal vertical sliver.
   - **Slide** above the frame, foreshortened — much shorter than in side
     view (it's pointing away from us). Top surface is the dominant face;
     side surfaces of the slide read as thin dark edges.
   - **Rear sight** at the *near* end of the slide (top-of-grip side, i.e.
     the bottom of the slide rectangle in sprite coordinates). Small
     squared notch with a bright nub, warm-yellow accent.
   - **Front sight + muzzle** at the *far* end of the slide (top of the
     slide rectangle in sprite coordinates). Front-sight nub centered on
     the slide; muzzle exit is the dark hole below the front sight.
   - **No hands, no forearms, no arm.** Same as the current sprite.

5. **Cant (slight tilt, lower-right pose).** The slide's vertical axis is
   not exactly vertical — tilt it by ~6-10° so the muzzle (top of slide)
   sits slightly to the *left* of the grip (bottom of slide) in
   sprite-local coordinates. This sells "held in the right hand, pointing
   toward screen center". The cant must be a single skew applied during
   generation (a per-row x-offset for slide and frame, computed once),
   not a runtime rotation. Grip stays vertical-ish (the hand wouldn't tilt
   with the slide); only the slide + frame + sights are canted.

6. **Screen anchor — lower-right (Doom-style).** Replace the centered
   anchor at `index.html:2375-2376`:
   ```js
   const baseX = (W - GUN_W) >> 1;
   const baseY = H - GUN_H + 4;
   ```
   with a lower-right anchor:
   ```js
   const baseX = ((W - GUN_W) >> 1) + GUN_RIGHT_SHIFT;
   const baseY = H - GUN_H + 4;
   ```
   where `GUN_RIGHT_SHIFT` is a new constant, `GUN_RIGHT_SHIFT = 32`
   (recommended; `[24..40]` acceptable). Co-locate it with the other gun
   constants at `index.html:72-78`. The vertical anchor stays the same —
   bottom of grip sits below the screen edge, hand off-frame.

7. **Barrel exit point relocates.** The current flash anchor at
   `drawGunViewmodel` (`index.html:2396-2397`):
   ```js
   const barrelX = dx + (GUN_W - 4);
   const barrelY = dy + 16;
   ```
   was the *right edge* of a side-profile slide. In the new layout the
   muzzle is at the *top* of the slide. New anchor:
   ```js
   const barrelX = dx + BARREL_X;
   const barrelY = dy + BARREL_Y;
   ```
   where `BARREL_X` / `BARREL_Y` are constants derived from where
   `buildGun()` actually placed the front-sight / muzzle hole. Place them
   next to `GUN_W` / `GUN_H`. With the recommended sizing
   (`GUN_W = 72`, `GUN_H = 96`) and a centered+canted slide, reasonable
   values are `BARREL_X ≈ 30`, `BARREL_Y ≈ 8`. The flash sprite is still
   centered on this point (`fdx = barrelX - (FLASH_W >> 1)` etc.), and
   it still uses saturating-add — both unchanged.

8. **Kick directions revisit.** `getKickOffsets` (`index.html:2338-2364`)
   currently returns `kickX = -2, kickY = -10` during a `'shot'` kick.
   Negative-Y (gun jumps up on screen) is correct for the new view.
   `kickX = -2` (small leftward shift) reads acceptably for the canted
   right pose — keep it. **Do not retune** unless the resulting motion
   looks visibly wrong; the kick magnitudes were tuned in
   `gun-viewmodel` AC #7 and are out-of-scope for this re-author.
   `KICK_UP_MS`, `KICK_RECOVER_MS`, dry-fire magnitudes, the `kicking →
   recovering → idle` state transitions, and `flashFrame` cycling all
   stay byte-identical.

9. **Bob math unchanged.** `bobPhase`, `bobIntensity`, the
   `bobOffX = sin(bobPhase) * 3 * bobIntensity` /
   `bobOffY = -|sin(bobPhase * 2)| * 2 * bobIntensity` formulas, the
   `BOB_SPEED = 8.0`, the easing-toward-rest behavior, and the smoothing
   time constant on `bobIntensity` are all preserved. The bob is applied
   to the sprite as a whole — it doesn't matter which direction the gun
   points; a small horizontal sway + vertical bounce reads correctly for
   either pose.

10. **Render ordering unchanged.** The gun continues to blit *after* the
    vignette pass (`drawGunViewmodel()` at `index.html:1675`, after the
    vignette block ends around `index.html:1670`), so the gun is HUD-class
    and never darkened at corners. No `lightingEnabled` gating is added or
    removed. `drawSprites`, the wall column loop, `castFloorCeiling`, the
    enemy muzzle-flash overlay, the crosshair, and the HUD overlays all
    keep their current order.

11. **Death-freeze still hides the gun.** `if (player.hp <= 0) return;` at
    the top of `drawGunViewmodel` (`index.html:2370`) stays. Kick state
    machine keeps ticking through death (existing behavior at
    `index.html:346-349`); only the blit is suppressed.

12. **Hitscan integration unchanged.** `onPlayerFire('shot')` /
    `onPlayerFire('dry')` are still called from the existing fire path.
    Do not modify `fireShot` or its callers; the gun viewmodel only
    *consumes* the fire event. Crosshair behavior, ammo HUD, hit-tint
    crosshair, and the legacy `muzzleFlashUntil` overlay (the lower-center
    flat-color additive flash at `index.html:1688+`) are all out of
    scope — leave them exactly as they are.

13. **Performance.** ≥ 30 FPS at 480×270 still holds in both lighting
    modes with the procedurally-generated dungeon and all initial enemies
    alive. The gun blit is at most `GUN_W * GUN_H = 9,216` pixel ops per
    frame (with the recommended 72×96) — comparable to the current 7,680.
    The flash blit is unchanged at `FLASH_W * FLASH_H = 576` pixels and
    only when active. Keep the alpha-test branch in the inner loop
    (`if (sc === 0) continue;`) and stay in `Uint32Array` for both
    sprite and destination.

14. **Determinism.** Page reload produces a byte-identical first frame.
    Same regression test as the original `gun-viewmodel` spec —
    `Math.random()` is the failure mode to watch for.

15. **No regressions.** Every previously-shipped behavior continues to
    work: walking, mouse-look, pointer lock, FPS counter, sprite z-buffer
    occlusion, click-to-fire hitscan, ammo / kills HUD, contact damage,
    death freeze + `R` reset, level-exit progression, audio, minimap,
    atmosphere lighting (L toggle), wall / floor / ceiling textures, the
    enemy muzzle flashes, dmg-flash + dmg-arrow + kill-pop. The
    viewmodel only changes its own pixels and anchor; nothing else
    reads from the gun sprite or the gun's screen position.

16. **No new console errors or warnings.** A 60-second session covering
    walking through every reachable cell, firing through a full magazine,
    dry-firing repeatedly, dying and pressing `R`, toggling L, and
    pressing N to regenerate — all clean.

17. **Visual identity bar.** A player should immediately read the new
    sprite as "a pistol I'm holding and pointing forward", not as
    "an abstract shape" or "the side of a gun". Front sight is visible
    above the slide; rear sight is visible at the back of the slide;
    grip reads as wood-brown / checkered; muzzle hole is a clear dark
    spot at the front of the slide; the muzzle flash, when it fires,
    appears centered on that hole. Swap-test: take a screenshot before
    and after this task — anyone seeing the after-shot should describe
    it as "first-person view down a pistol", not "side view of a pistol".

    helpers (`index.html:2169-2183`) — they're geometry-agnostic.
  - `index.html:2369-2417` — `drawGunViewmodel`. Two changes only:
    `baseX` formula gains `+ GUN_RIGHT_SHIFT`; `barrelX` / `barrelY`
    use the new `BARREL_X` / `BARREL_Y` constants. Everything else in
    the function is untouched.
  - **Nothing else.** `tickKick`, `getKickOffsets`, `onPlayerFire`,
    `buildFlash1`, `buildFlash2`, the bob accumulator in `update`, the
    fire hooks in the hitscan path — all stay verbatim.

- **`buildGun()` sketch — back view, canted slightly right.**
  Shapes are written from far (top of sprite) to near (bottom). Apply
  the cant as a per-row x-offset:
  `const cant = ((y - SLIDE_TOP_Y) * CANT_NUM / CANT_DEN) | 0;`
  with `CANT_NUM / CANT_DEN ≈ 1/6` (one px left per six rows down).

  ```js
  function buildGun() {
    const PALETTE = {
      outline:  rgba32( 20,  18,  22),
      bodyDark: rgba32( 60,  60,  70),
      bodyMid:  rgba32(100, 100, 110),
      bodyHi:   rgba32(170, 170, 180),
      grip:     rgba32( 95,  60,  35),
      gripDark: rgba32( 55,  35,  20),
      sight:    rgba32(220, 200,  80),
    };
    const spr = new Uint32Array(GUN_W * GUN_H);
    const cx = GUN_W >> 1;

    // 1. Slide top face (foreshortened rectangle near the top of the sprite).
    //    Width ~28, height ~26 with cant. Anchor center-x at `cx`.
    //    Iterate row-by-row applying `cant` so the slide tips left as we
    //    move down (i.e., the muzzle at the TOP is leftmost-shifted? No —
    //    we want the muzzle slightly LEFT of the rear-sight, which means
    //    the top of the slide is shifted LEFT relative to the bottom).
    //    Implementation: cant *increases* as y decreases (toward the top),
    //    so subtract from x for upper rows.
    //
    //    Then layer:
    //    - body fill (bodyMid)
    //    - top highlight strip (bodyHi)
    //    - dark side strips on the left/right edges of the slide rect
    //      (these are the slide's vertical sides as seen from above-rear)
    //    - outline pass
    //
    // 2. Rear sight (small notched block at the slide's NEAR end —
    //    bottom of the slide rect in sprite coords). Two outlined nubs
    //    with a notch between them, sight color on top.
    //
    // 3. Front sight (small nub at the slide's FAR end — top of the slide
    //    rect). Single bright squared dot in `sight` color, outlined.
    //
    // 4. Muzzle hole. A dark (or transparent) 3-4 px circle just BELOW
    //    the front sight on the slide's far face. This is the visible
    //    barrel exit and is what BARREL_X / BARREL_Y point at.
    //
    // 5. Frame / trigger guard. A wider rectangle below the slide, also
    //    canted. The trigger guard hangs off the front (top-ish) of the
    //    frame as an outlined hollow shape. Trigger inside, vertical
    //    sliver in `bodyMid`.
    //
    // 6. Grip. Vertical rectangle (NOT canted — the hand wouldn't tilt
    //    with the slide), brown, with checkering stippled every 3rd row
    //    in `gripDark`. Slightly narrower than the frame so the frame
    //    visibly overhangs at the top of the grip. Bottom edge can sit
    //    at y = GUN_H - 1 (the existing baseY = H - GUN_H + 4 puts the
    //    last 4 rows off-screen — good).
    //
    // 7. Outline pass on every shape using `gunOutlineRect`.
    //
    // No Math.random anywhere. No textures. Pure rectangles + a few
    // single-pixel sets for the muzzle hole and rear-sight notch.
  }
  ```

  The implementer should iterate the visual until it reads as a back-view
  pistol — pixel-art rules out perfect realism. The two concrete tests
  are: (a) the muzzle hole is visible and is the dark spot the flash
  centers on; (b) the silhouette reads top-to-bottom as muzzle → slide →
  frame → grip, not left-to-right.

- **Cant amount.** ~6-10° feels right; more than ~12° starts to look
  like the gun is being held sideways. Implement as integer pixel skew
  (`(rowOffset / 6) | 0` etc.) so the result is byte-identical across
  reloads.

- **Why `GUN_RIGHT_SHIFT = 32` (not 24, not 40).** With `W = 480` and a
  72-pixel-wide sprite, centered would put the gun at `x ∈ [204, 276]`
  — straddling the crosshair at `x = 240`. Doom's pistol sits visibly
  right of the crosshair, in the right hand. Shifting by `32` puts the
  gun at `x ∈ [236, 308]`, with the gun's left edge just brushing the
  crosshair and the gun's centerline at `x = 272` — about 6.7% right of
  center, which reads as "lower-right" without the gun escaping the
  bottom-center HUD zone. `24` is too central; `40` starts to crowd the
  right edge of the screen.

- **Why no rotation in code.** A run-time `ctx.rotate` would force a
  separate canvas blit (we're putting pixels into `buf32` directly). A
  baked-in cant via per-row x-offset stays in the existing pixel pipeline
  and stays integer-only. The visual result is indistinguishable at this
  resolution.

- **Where to fetch the original spec for context:**
  `specs/gun-viewmodel.md` (committed). The bob/kick/flash mechanics
  there still apply verbatim — only `AC #2` (sprite layout) and the
  derived screen anchor / barrel-exit point are superseded by this task.

- **Common pitfalls:**
  - **Forgetting to update the flash anchor.** If `BARREL_X` / `BARREL_Y`
    still point at `(GUN_W - 4, 16)`-style coordinates from the old
    side-view, the muzzle flash appears off the gun, in the lower-right
    *blank space* of the sprite. Visible immediately on first fire.
  - **Muzzle flash hidden behind slide.** The flash is anchored on the
    barrel-exit point and centered there with `FLASH_W / FLASH_H = 24`.
    If `BARREL_Y` is too low (deep inside the slide rather than at its
    far face), half the flash is *under* the slide pixels and looks
    truncated. Keep `BARREL_Y ≤ 10` so the flash blooms above the
    slide top.
  - **Cant going the wrong way.** A right-handed Doom-pose has the
    muzzle (top of slide) shifted *left* relative to the grip
    (bottom of slide). If your skew shifts the muzzle right instead,
    the gun reads as held in the *left* hand — invert the cant sign.
  - **Grip clipped above the bottom edge.** With `gunY = H - GUN_H + 4`,
    the bottom 4 rows of the sprite are off-screen. If your grip ends
    before `y = GUN_H - 1`, the bottom of the grip is visible and the
    "hand off-frame" illusion breaks. Draw the grip down to at least
    `y = GUN_H - 1`.
  - **`Math.random` slipping into a generator.** Forbidden by the
    determinism contract. Use only deterministic ops in `buildGun`.
  - **Re-tuning kick / bob magnitudes.** Out of scope. If the new view
    *looks* like the kick is wrong, it's almost certainly the sprite
    orientation, not the kick numbers — kick was tuned to reach
    visibly higher in side-profile, and the same `-10 px` Y motion
    reads correctly in back view too. Don't change `kickX` / `kickY`
    unless the result is genuinely visually broken; if it is, flag as
    a follow-up.
  - **Editing `specs/gun-viewmodel.md`.** Don't. The original spec is
    immutable history; this task supersedes its `AC #2` and derived
    anchor/barrel-exit clauses without rewriting it.
